This site exists to argue with its engine. The main corpus — twenty-seven
years of a real blog — is one site, and one site cannot contradict the
assumptions baked into the code that renders it.

So this one is built to contradict:

- a photo section with nothing but images and aspect ratios,
- a manual that wants to render as a tree,
- recipes that are rows in a database wearing prose,
- and notes like this one, which mostly exist to fill listings.

If a feature works here and on the main site, it is probably real.
