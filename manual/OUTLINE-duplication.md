# Duplication inside OUTLINE.md — noted, not acted on

Flagged during the doc compression pass by three separate readers of the
outline. Nothing here has been changed. The outline's own voice rule 3 makes
some repetition deliberate ("one law, introduced once, referenced forever"), so
each entry is marked for whether it looks like reinforcement or drift.

## Probably real — worth consolidating

- **"Ship a shell, own the frame"** — ch. 13 and ch. 14 both state the whole
  principle, not a reference to it. Two readers flagged this one independently.
- **Per-row theme cascade** — ch. 13 gives the cascade (front matter → rule
  default → `[site] theme` → `default` dir → base), then ch. 14 restates it
  when teaching theme writing. One statement plus "writing themes uses the same
  cascade" would do.
- **Token contract** — spans ch. 13 (subthemes), ch. 14 (the contract) and
  ch. 15 (cascade order and token families). Three partial statements; one
  authoritative one with two pointers reads better.
- **Variant mechanism** — introduced ch. 16, with example usage repeated in
  ch. 13 and ch. 14. One deep example could replace three shallow ones.
- **Breadcrumbs** — three sources taught in three places: mode-B landing chains
  (ch. 18), tree ancestors (ch. 22), grouped-route keys (ch. 8). A single
  "where a crumb comes from" section would make the three legible as one idea.

## Probably deliberate — leave alone

- **The precedence law** — ch. 4 states it, ch. 8/13/14/22 reference it. This is
  voice rule 3 working as designed. (Note: §4d now states it better than ch. 4
  does — three shapes, three rules — so ch. 4 should adopt that framing rather
  than being deduplicated.)
- **Drafts, flags and profiles** — ch. 4 frames, ch. 23 mechanics, ch. 24
  migration, ch. 29 search. Different aspects, appropriate split.
- **Link resolution** — ch. 10 teaches it, ch. 24 revisits under migration,
  ch. 25 under widgets. Different angles. One reader suggested bucket-climb
  resolution deserves its own depth, split out of ch. 10.
- **`shell:` two axes** — ch. 5 (route shells) and ch. 19 (row shells). The
  split is explained late; pulling the two-axes statement forward to ch. 5
  would help, but it is not duplication.
- **`.schema.toml`** — ch. 21, ch. 22, ch. 24. Suggested but not obviously
  needed: one unified "schema as annotation" chapter.
- **i18n** — ch. 13 incidental mention, ch. 30 full treatment. Fine.
