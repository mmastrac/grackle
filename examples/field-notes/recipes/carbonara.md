---
title: Spaghetti carbonara
course: dinner
servings: 2
minutes: 25
---
Guanciale, eggs, pecorino, pepper. No cream — the sauce is an emulsion,
not an addition.

## Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 100 g guanciale
- 2 whole eggs plus 1 yolk
- 50 g pecorino romano, finely grated
- black pepper, a lot

## Method

1. Render the guanciale slowly until crisp; keep the fat.
2. Whisk eggs, pecorino, and pepper into a paste.
3. Cook the pasta a minute short; move it to the pan off heat.
4. Add the paste and a splash of pasta water; toss until glossy.
