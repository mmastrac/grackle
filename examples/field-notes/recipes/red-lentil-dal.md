The tadka is the recipe; the lentils are the vehicle.

## Method

1. Simmer lentils with turmeric until collapsed.
2. Soften the aromatics separately.
3. Bloom the seeds and chilies in ghee until they pop.
4. Combine, salt, finish with cilantro.
