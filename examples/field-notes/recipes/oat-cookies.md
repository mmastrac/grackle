Brown the butter, chill the dough, do not skip either.

## Ingredients

- 225 g butter, browned and cooled
- 200 g brown sugar, 100 g white sugar
- 2 eggs, 1 tsp vanilla
- 250 g flour, 200 g rolled oats
- 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. Cream the cooled brown butter with both sugars.
2. Add eggs and vanilla; fold in the dry ingredients.
3. Chill at least an hour — overnight is better.
4. Bake at 180°C for 11–12 minutes; they should look underdone.
