Brown the butter, chill the dough, do not skip either.

## Method

1. Cream the cooled brown butter with both sugars.
2. Add eggs and vanilla; fold in the dry mix.
3. Chill at least an hour — overnight is better.
4. Bake at 180°C for 11–12 minutes; they should look underdone.
