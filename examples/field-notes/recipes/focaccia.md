---
title: Overnight focaccia
course: baking
servings: 8
minutes: 40
---
High hydration, no kneading, all patience.

## Ingredients

- 500 g bread flour
- 420 g water
- 10 g salt
- 4 g instant yeast
- olive oil, flaky salt, rosemary

## Method

1. Mix everything but the toppings; rest 30 minutes.
2. Four sets of folds over two hours, then refrigerate overnight.
3. Pour into an oiled pan, proof two hours, dimple with oiled fingers.
4. Bake at 230°C for ~22 minutes.
