---
title: When the tags go missing
tags: [writing]
---
A post with no tags should not leave an empty box in the margin. The hole
for the list is simply gone from the HTML — not hidden, not zero-height,
gone. That is why a theme can style `.tags` without also styling its empty
state.

The catch is wrappers you invent yourself. Group two optional bits in a
`<div>` of your own and the div survives them both.
