Four themes, one content tree. Every page under a theme's prefix carries
that theme, so the same shapes render four ways and you can keep two tabs
open and diff them by eye.

This landing has no theme of its own, which is why it looks like nothing:
it renders through the **null theme** — no shell, no fragments, no CSS,
just the canonical markup the engine emits. That is the floor every theme
here is built up from.

- **[ledger](ledger/index.md)** — one warm serif column, dark mode.
- **[marginalia](marginalia/index.md)** — text plus a margin column.
- **[terminal](terminal/index.md)** — monospace, dark-first, nothing rounded.
- **[atlas](atlas/index.md)** — sticky section tree, cards, masonry.

Each theme's landing links on to its own notes listing, section tree and
shelf.
