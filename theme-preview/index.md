Six themes, **one content tree**. The themes are an axis (q53): every post,
guide page and shelf entry below is a single row published at six routes, each
rendering through a different theme. This page is one of those rows — you are
reading its `vanilla` member.

The same shapes six ways, so you can keep two tabs open and diff them by eye.
Each theme's notes listing, section tree, shelf and wall:

- **vanilla** — [notes](view:vanilla_notes) · [shelf](view:vanilla_shelf) · [wall](view:vanilla_wall)
- **ledger** — [notes](view:ledger_notes) · [shelf](view:ledger_shelf) · [wall](view:ledger_wall)
- **marginalia** — [notes](view:marginalia_notes) · [shelf](view:marginalia_shelf) · [wall](view:marginalia_wall)
- **terminal** — [notes](view:terminal_notes) · [shelf](view:terminal_shelf) · [wall](view:terminal_wall)
- **atlas** — [notes](view:atlas_notes) · [shelf](view:atlas_shelf) · [wall](view:atlas_wall)
- **miroir** — [notes](view:miroir_notes) · [shelf](view:miroir_shelf) · [wall](view:miroir_wall)

An axis member has no link syntax of its own yet: a link resolves to a row,
and a row answers with its canonical URL. Linking to a *specific* member — the
ledger rendering of this page — is not expressible, which is why the list above
names landing views rather than members.
