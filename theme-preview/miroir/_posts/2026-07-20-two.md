---
title: An empty part deletes its element
tags: [parts]
---
A fragment has no conditional syntax because nothing needs one. A document with no tags emits no tag list, and the `<p>` that would have held it is gone from the output — not hidden, gone.

The catch is that this applies to *parts*, not to wrappers a fragment invents. Group two parts in a `<div>` of your own and the div survives them both.
